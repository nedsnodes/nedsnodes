# ned-box runs out of reclaimable memory, so systemd-oomd kills agent-seats and readings by `nn-systems/general-tools/bash-guards-verdict-changes-between-git-refs-on-recorded-commands-check.py` time out

## What is wrong

Five times from 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-22, systemd-oomd, Ubuntu's memory-pressure killer, has killed an agent-seat's whole tmux pane on ned-box, `claude` included. The seat loses whatever it had not yet saved, and the agent-crash-watcher relaunches it. On 2026-10-21 the agent-seat it killed was reboot-test, which was idle at the time.

The first three kills came 30 to 63 seconds after merge-lane-2 started `nn-systems/general-tools/bash-guards-verdict-changes-between-git-refs-on-recorded-commands-check.py`. The same load made five of the check's readings run past their 20 s timeout on [Search-file guard's time-limit and error note reuses its plain next-step line](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/pull/1825). Rerun with a 60 s timeout, each of those readings took 1 s. [Bash-guards replay check reads a timed-out reading once more](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/pull/1833) adds a retry for such timeouts. This issue is for the cause.

The other two kills, on 2026-10-22 at 02:00:52Z and 10:01:02Z, followed no replay check. Each came within 61 seconds of the hourly snapshot that timeshift, ned-box's system backup, starts at the top of every hour: an rsync of `/`, `/home/nedlern` included, that walks 1.6 million files. In the hour before the first, no agent-seat started heavy work, and systemd-oomd also killed the login screen in another user's slice, so the pressure was machine-wide. Before the second, merge-lane-2 had started a full run of every test suite three minutes earlier. The first kill, on 2026-10-17, also came 44 seconds after a timeshift snapshot started.

On 2026-11-27 systemd-oomd killed seven more panes, in two bursts, while ned-box still had no swap. Timeshift's hourly snapshot started at 10:00:01Z, and its rsync ran from 10:00:03Z to 10:06:22Z; the first burst fell inside that rsync:

- At 10:03:40Z it killed `tmux-spawn-975ad63d-3aa9-4b46-8a27-92140066c387.scope`, which used 435.5M, with pressure on `user@1000.service` at 91.68%.
- At 10:03:56Z it killed `tmux-spawn-42f4abeb-fc14-46b2-a403-74c042da3cf4.scope`, which used 733M, at 96.38%. agent-crash-watcher's decisions log records merge-lane-1 and reboot-test as dying at about 10:03:32Z and 10:03:52Z, and relaunches both at 10:05:01Z.
- At 10:06:03Z it killed `tmux-spawn-6eae874d-029b-4a69-ab3a-48d9b0f12b2e.scope`, which used 421.8M, at 60.36%. systemd had started that pane at 10:05:02Z, so it was one of those two relaunches; the other, `tmux-spawn-00ed1a2a-9ddc-4924-be4b-134edd4f8ffe.scope`, started at 10:05:12Z. The watcher then records merge-lane-1 as down again and does not relaunch it, because it had already relaunched it less than an hour before.

The second burst came about half an hour later, after the timeshift run that started at 10:00:01Z had exited at 10:14:41Z: from 10:34:52Z to 10:37:52Z it killed four more scopes, which used from 297.9M to 6.6G, `tmux-spawn-00ed1a2a-…` first. The watcher records MD-skills, reboot-test, merge-lane-3 and cold-read-improvement as dying between 10:34:50Z and 10:37:39Z. What started this burst is not identified. Four `run-all-test-suites-*` log directories under `/tmp` were created between 10:30:43Z and 10:34:11Z and last written between 10:32:46Z and 10:39:35Z, so four runs of `scripts/run-all-test-suites.py` were under way; which agent-seat started them, and whether a replay check also ran, was not checked.

ned-box has had a 32G swap file, `/swapfile`, since 11:22:28Z, the first swap the kernel added since ned-box booted on 2026-11-21; `/etc/fstab` turns it on at every boot. The journal shows no systemd-oomd kill from 10:37:52Z to 12:21Z. In that time one snapshot ran, from 11:00:01Z to 12:18:07Z, its rsync from 11:00:09Z to 11:46:38Z; swap arrived 22 minutes into that rsync, so no snapshot has yet run from start to end with swap in place. The 12:00Z snapshot did not run, because the 11:00Z one was still going.

The sources for 2026-11-27 are copied to nedlern@ned-box:/home/nedlern/nedsnodes-logs/seats/reboot-test/ned-box-oomd-kills-2026-11-27/, because the journal rotates and timeshift deletes its own logs. They came from these places on ned-box:

- systemd-oomd's kills: `journalctl --utc --since '2026-11-27 09:55 UTC' --until '2026-11-27 10:40 UTC' -u systemd-oomd`; for the absence of later kills, `journalctl --utc --since '2026-11-27 10:37:53 UTC' -u systemd-oomd | grep Killed`. Without the ` UTC` suffix, journalctl reads `--since` and `--until` as ned-box's local time, America/Los_Angeles, even under `--utc`, and finds no entries.
- When the panes started: the `Started tmux-spawn-…scope` lines in the same journal.
- When timeshift started: the journal's `CRON` line `(root) CMD (timeshift --check --scripted)` at 10:00:01Z, from `/etc/cron.d/timeshift-hourly`. How long each snapshot ran: `/var/log/timeshift/2026-11-27_02-00-01_backup.log`, `2026-11-27_03-00-01_backup.log` and `2026-11-27_04-00-01_backup.log`, whose file names and times are in local time.
- The watcher's records: nedlern@ned-box:/home/nedlern/nedsnodes-logs/handoff-agent-seat-died-without-session-handoff-restart-watcher/handoff-agent-seat-died-without-session-handoff-restart-watcher-decisions.jsonl.
- The swap: `swapon --show`, the swap line in `/etc/fstab`, and the kernel's `Adding 33554428k swap on /swapfile` line, the only such line in `journalctl -b`.
- The test-suite runs: the creation and modification times `stat` gives for their log directories.

The trace of the October kills, with every measurement and its source, is at nedlern@ned-box:/home/nedlern/nedsnodes-logs/seats/reboot-test/ned-box-oomd-kills-trace-2026-10-22/trace.md. In short, as measured on 2026-10-22:

- ned-box has 59.6G of RAM, and had no swap until 2026-11-27. `/tmp` is a tmpfs, which keeps its files in RAM. It held 21G: 12G of agent-session scratchpads, 4.8G in 34 replay-check scratch directories the check left behind, and 3.1G in 562 `run-all-test-suites-*` log directories. Without swap, none of those 21G could be reclaimed, and killing a pane freed none of them.
- Each replay-check run reads 17.5G of transcripts and starts 8 worker processes. It does not go through the free-memory admission that `scripts/run-all-test-suites.py` (class `MachineWideTestSuiteAdmission`) and mutation testing use.
- That admission counts page cache as free memory, and does not count the tmpfs's pages as used.
- systemd-oomd kills the scope with the most reclaim activity, as oomd.conf(5) on ned-box (`man oomd.conf`, under `DefaultMemoryPressureLimit=`) says. That is a long-lived agent-seat, not the process that applied the pressure.
- timeshift runs as root from `/etc/cron.d/timeshift-hourly`, in rsync mode, and keeps 24 hourly snapshots; changing it needs root. No step under What to do covers timeshift yet.

## Why it matters

An agent-seat killed this way loses its unsaved work: a subagent's uncommitted changes, a running test or mutation run, messages owed to other agent-seats. Every seat on ned-box is at risk whenever the replay check or timeshift's hourly snapshot runs. The pressure also slows every guard and test running at the same moment, and tests sized to an idle machine then fail; that cause is [Tests that time work against a wall-clock limit sized to an idle machine fail when ned-box is busy](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/issues/1830).

## What to do

1. The replay check waits for the free-memory admission before it reads any transcript, because the kills came while it was reading them, before its workers started. The build decides how the check takes part in `MachineWideTestSuiteAdmission`, which today admits test suites, and measures which lowers the pressure more: reading the transcripts without leaving them in page cache, or fewer than its 8 workers. Its scratch directory goes on disk, not in `/tmp`, and is removed whenever the check exits; when a verdict changed or a reading failed, the files a reader needs to see why are first copied to the log-store. reboot-test owns the replay-check topic and builds this step first.
2. Large scratch files and logs move from `/tmp` to disk, with pruning: the `run-all-test-suites-*` log directories and the git worktrees that `scripts/mutation-testing-changed-lines-quick-or-full-pass-survivors.py` makes. Agent-session temp folders are done: [ned-box agent-sessions keep Claude Code's temp folders under ~/.cache on the main disk; a daily job deletes those of exited sessions](https://github.com/nedsnodes/nedsnodes/pull/2010) has `nn-systems/handoff/handoff-supervisor.py` give each agent-session it launches on ned-box `CLAUDE_CODE_TMPDIR=/home/nedlern/.cache/claude-code-tmp`, so Claude Code makes each agent-session's folder, its scratchpad and background-task output, under `/home/nedlern/.cache/claude-code-tmp/claude-1000/` instead of `/tmp/claude-1000/`. Timeshift's hourly snapshot leaves out `/home/nedlern/.cache/**`, so these folders stay out of it. An agent-session started before that change keeps using `/tmp` until it restarts. `nn-systems/general-tools/claude-code-temp-folders-of-exited-agent-sessions-delete.py`, run by cron on ned-box at 07:30 daily, deletes the folders of exited agent-sessions in both places. What remains of this step is the test-suite log directories and the mutation-testing worktrees.
3. The free-memory admission counts the tmpfs's pages (`Shmem` in `/proc/meminfo`) as used.
4. Keep a record of memory over time on ned-box, at an interval short enough to trace the next kill. sysstat already records memory every 10 minutes and keeps 7 days (`OnCalendar=*:00/10` in `sysstat-collect.timer`, `HISTORY=7` in `/etc/sysstat/sysstat`; `/var/log/sysstat` held `sa15` to `sa23` on 2026-10-23, so every kill day has a record). sysstat as it stands does not finish this step: each kill came 45 to 84 seconds after a sample, so the samples show the machine before the pressure and then averaged over the next ten minutes, not the pressure itself, as the trace's sar tables show. What remains is a 1-minute interval. That needs root, so the agent gives the user the command to run.

Next action: reboot-test builds step 1 as one pull request. Steps 2 to 4 each go to the agent-seat that last changed the program involved, as `git log` shows, or to the user for what needs root, and each is tracked in this GHI-MD until it is done.
